# Expense Reimbursement Policy (FIN-POL-07)

Effective: 2025-03-01 | Owner: Corporate Finance

## Eligible expenses

Business travel, client entertainment (pre-approved), professional development courses under **$2,000**, and office supplies over the team card limit.

## Receipts and timing

- Expenses above **$25** require an itemized receipt.
- Submit expenses in Concur within **30 days** of the transaction date.
- Late submissions (>30 days) need director approval; submissions after **90 days** are denied unless CFO exception.

## Travel

- Economy class for flights under 6 hours; premium economy allowed for longer flights with manager approval.
- Hotels capped at **$275/night** in Tier-1 cities unless pre-approved.
- Per diem meals: **$75/day** domestic, **$100/day** international.

## Prohibited

Personal entertainment, fines, first-class upgrades without CFO approval, and gift cards purchased as "team morale" without HR co-approval.

## Approval matrix

| Amount | Approver |
|--------|----------|
| ≤ $500 | Manager |
| $501–$5,000 | Director |
| > $5,000 | VP Finance |
