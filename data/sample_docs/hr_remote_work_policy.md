# Remote Work Policy (HR-POL-12)

Effective: 2025-01-01 | Owner: People Operations

## Eligibility

Full-time employees with at least 90 days of continuous service may request a remote or hybrid arrangement. Contractors require VP approval.

## Hybrid baseline

- Default hybrid schedule is **3 days in office / 2 days remote** per week.
- Core collaboration hours are **10:00–15:00 local time** on in-office days.
- Managers may approve fully remote status for roles designated as "location-flexible" in the job catalog.

## Equipment

The company provides a laptop and stipend of **$750** every 24 months for home-office setup. Employees must use MFA on all corporate accounts.

## Security

Remote workers must:
1. Connect via the corporate VPN for systems marked Restricted.
2. Never store customer PII on personal devices.
3. Report lost devices to IT Security within **4 hours**.

## Exceptions

Exceptions to the hybrid baseline require written approval from the employee's director and HR Business Partner. Temporary exceptions (travel, medical) may be granted for up to **30 calendar days** without director sign-off if the manager notifies HR.
